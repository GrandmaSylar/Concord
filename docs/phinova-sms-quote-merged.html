01 Overview
Concord gives you a clean, working tool that handles everything you need — sending messages, managing contacts, and setting up automated reminders. Here's what's included:
Clean, Mobile-Friendly Design
Included
A polished dashboard that works beautifully on both phone and desktop. No clutter — just the tools you need, laid out in a way that's easy to pick up and use from day one.
Bulk SMS Sending
Included
Send messages to your full contact list or paste in a custom group of numbers. You'll see delivery confirmations after every send so you always know what went through.
Message Templates
Included
Save your most-used messages as templates — promotions, appointment reminders, alerts — so your team isn't retyping the same thing every time.
Automated Reminders
Included
Schedule messages to go out automatically on birthdays, anniversaries, or appointment dates. Set it once and let the system handle it.
Contact Management
Included
Upload your contact list, organise people into groups, and keep everything in one place. Import from a spreadsheet in seconds.
Login & User Access
Included
Secure login with password protection. You can set up separate access levels — admins can manage everything, while regular users have a more limited view.
Hosting & Setup
Included
Everything lives on one reliable hosting platform. We handle the setup, the security certificate (so your site runs on https://), and the go-live process. You don't touch anything technical.
Have something extra in mind? Things like WhatsApp messaging, email campaigns, or connecting with your existing tools can all be scoped and quoted separately — just ask.
