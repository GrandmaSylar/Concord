2. Managing Contacts
Add, import, search, and organise your contact list
▼

The Contacts page is your central address book. Every person you want to send an SMS to must exist here (or be entered as a temporary number on the Send page).

Adding a Single Contact

  1. Click Add Contact at the top-right of the Contacts page.
  2. Fill in the contact's name and phone number (include country code, e.g. +233201234567).
  3. Optionally assign a Group to organise contacts (e.g. "Executives", "Ward Members").
  4. Click Save. The contact appears in the list immediately.

Importing Contacts from a CSV File

You can upload hundreds or thousands of contacts at once using a CSV file.

  1. Prepare a spreadsheet with at minimum two columns: name and phone. An optional group column is also supported.
  2. Export or save the file as .csv (Comma Separated Values).
  3. On the Contacts page, click Import CSV.
  4. Select your file. The platform will preview the import and show how many contacts will be added.
  5. Confirm the import. Duplicate phone numbers are skipped automatically.
Your CSV header row should be exactly: name,phone or name,phone,group. Extra columns are ignored.

Searching and Filtering

Use the search bar at the top of the Contacts page to find contacts by name or phone number. Use the Group dropdown to filter by a specific group. Pagination handles large lists automatically in sets of 50.

Contact Fields Reference

FieldRequired?Notes
NameRequiredFull name. Used in personalisation tags like [Fullname].
PhoneRequiredMust include country code. E.g. +233201234567.
GroupOptionalFree-text label to group contacts for filtered sending.
Phone numbers without a country code may fail to deliver. Always prefix with + and the country code (e.g. Ghana = +233).

Filter, select, and message your constituency contacts with precision

Selecting Contacts and Sending

  1. Apply any combination of filters and search to narrow the list.
  2. Click individual rows to select them, or use the header checkbox to select the entire visible page.
  3. Click Select All Matching (N) to select every contact that matches your current filters — even across multiple pages.
  4. Check the Exclude contacts without phone checkbox to remove unreachable contacts automatically.
  5. When satisfied, click Send SMS to Selected in the sticky footer bar. You will be taken directly to the Send page with all selected contacts pre-loaded.
Contacts highlighted in amber have no phone number and cannot receive SMS. The platform automatically excludes them from the SMS-ready count.

4. Sending Bulk SMS
Compose, personalise, and dispatch messages to any number of recipients
▼

The Send SMS page is the core of the platform. It is split into two steps: Select Recipients and Compose Message.

Step 1 — Select Recipients

You can select recipients in three ways:

  • From the contact list — use the search bar, sub-area filter, position filter, and group filter to narrow the list, then tick the contacts you want.
  • Select All Matching (N) — selects every contact matching your current search/filter across all pages, up to the full database.
  • From the Constituency tab — pre-populates the list automatically when you click "Send SMS to Selected" there. A blue banner confirms how many contacts were imported.
The selected count badge in the top-right of the section updates live. The Compose section below remains locked until at least one recipient is chosen.

Temporary Numbers

Need to send to a number that isn't in your contact list? Use the Temporary Numbers field below the contact table. Paste or type numbers separated by commas or semicolons. These messages are sent immediately but the numbers are not saved to your contacts. Personalisation tags do not apply to temporary numbers.

Character Count and SMS Parts

The counter below the message box shows the number of characters typed and how many SMS "parts" will be used. Standard SMS supports 160 characters per part. Messages longer than 160 characters automatically span multiple parts — each part consumes one credit per recipient.

Sending the Campaign

  1. Review the recipient count and your message in the preview panel.
  2. Click Send Campaign.
  3. A confirmation dialog shows the Sender ID and total recipient count. Read carefully before confirming.
  4. Click Yes, Send Messages. The messages are immediately queued for dispatch.
  5. A success toast confirms how many messages were queued. You can monitor delivery progress from the Dashboard or Reports page.

Sending is irreversible once confirmed. Always review the recipient count and message preview before clicking "Yes, Send Messages".

5. Message Templates
Save and reuse your best messages to save time on every campaign
▼

Templates let you save frequently-used messages so you never have to type the same text twice. They can include personalisation tags.

Creating a Template

  1. Navigate to Templates in the sidebar.
  2. Click New Template.
  3. Give the template a descriptive Name (e.g. "Election Day Reminder").
  4. Type your message in the Content field. You may include personalisation tags like [Firstname].
  5. Click Save Template.

Using a Template

On the Send SMS page, open the Use a Template dropdown and select a saved template. The message body is instantly populated. You can edit the text further before sending.

Editing and Deleting Templates

Each template card on the Templates page has Edit and Delete buttons. Editing opens the same form used for creation. Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no undo.

Keep template names short and descriptive — they appear in a dropdown on the Send page, so clarity matters when you have many templates.

6. Scheduled Reminders
Create messages that send automatically at the exact date and time you choose
▼

The Reminders page lets you schedule a message to be sent to a specific contact at a future date and time. The system checks for due reminders every minute and dispatches them automatically — no action needed from you at send time.

Creating a Reminder

  1. Click New Reminder on the Reminders page.
  2. Select a Contact from your contacts list. The contact must have a valid phone number.
  3. Type the message you want to send. Personalisation tags are supported.
  4. Set the Date and Time for delivery. The platform uses your local timezone — confirm the time is correct.
  5. Click Schedule Reminder. The reminder is saved and will appear on the Scheduled Queue page.

How Automatic Delivery Works

Every minute, the system automatically scans for reminders whose scheduled time has passed and whose status is still Pending. It dispatches those messages immediately. This happens entirely in the background — you do not need to be logged in for reminders to fire.

Reminder Statuses

Pending
Sent
Failed
  • Pending — scheduled for a future time, not yet sent.
  • Sent — successfully dispatched at the scheduled time.
  • Failed — dispatch was attempted but the message could not be delivered.
Schedule reminders at least 2 minutes ahead of the intended delivery time to ensure the system has time to process them in the next cycle.
Reminders are sent to a single contact per entry. To send a scheduled message to many people, create a reminder for each contact, or use the Send SMS page which also supports scheduling via the Reminders flow.

7. Scheduled Queue
View and manage all upcoming scheduled messages in one place
▼

The Scheduled page shows every reminder currently in the queue — past, present, and future. Use it to confirm your reminders were saved correctly and to cancel any you no longer want.

Reading the Queue

Each row in the queue shows:

  • Status badge — PENDING, SENT, or FAILED
  • Contact name and phone number
  • Message preview (truncated if long)
  • Scheduled time — the exact date and time the message was set to fire

Cancelling a Pending Reminder

Only Pending reminders can be cancelled. Click the red calendar icon on the right side of the row and confirm the prompt. Sent and failed reminders are read-only records for your reference.

Sent reminders remain visible in the queue as a permanent audit trail. They cannot be deleted from this screen.

8. Delivery Reports
Track every message sent — delivery status, timestamps, and recipients
▼

The Reports page is a complete log of every message the platform has ever sent. It is sorted with the most recent messages at the top.

Report Columns

ColumnDescription
RecipientThe phone number the message was sent to.
Message ContentThe body of the SMS (truncated for long messages).
StatusSent ✅ / Failed ❌ / Pending ⏳
Sent AtThe exact timestamp the message was dispatched.

Understanding Delivery Statuses

  • Sent — the message was accepted by the network and forwarded for final delivery to the handset.
  • Failed — the message could not be delivered. Common reasons: invalid phone number format, unreachable number, or network rejection.
  • Pending — the message is still in the queue and has not been dispatched yet.
Delivery status is updated automatically as the network reports back. A "Sent" message has been accepted by the carrier — it does not always mean the recipient has read it.

9. Developer Settings
Advanced configuration and testing tools — restricted access
▼

⚙️ Developer content is hidden by default. These settings are intended for administrators and technical operators only. Click the button to reveal or hide the full Developer Settings documentation.

The Developer Settings page requires a passphrase every time you visit it. It is intentionally hidden — access is gained by clicking the Concord logo in the sidebar 7 times rapidly. A password prompt will appear.
🔐 Access & Security

Every visit to the Developer Portal requires re-authentication — even if you just left the page. This includes using the browser's back button. The system tracks failed password attempts:

  • You have 5 attempts per window before a lockout is triggered.
  • Lockouts escalate — 1st lockout is 30 seconds, 2nd is 60 seconds, 3rd is 120 seconds, and so on (doubling each time).
  • A live countdown timer is shown during lockout. Attempt indicators (5 dots) fill red as failures accumulate.
  • Lockout state is tied to your browser session — closing the tab resets it.

The Theming Engine lets administrators change the platform's visual branding across all users instantly. Changes are stored in the database and reflected for every logged-in user on next page load.

📱 Test SMS Sender

Send a one-off test SMS to any phone number to verify that message delivery is working correctly. Enter the number with country code and click Send Test Message. The result (success or error) is displayed immediately below the button.

Use Test SMS after any configuration change to confirm the platform is still able to send messages before running a real campaign.
⚡ Simulation Playground (Stress Test)

The Simulation Playground lets you stress-test the message queue pipeline without sending real SMS messages. It creates simulated "pending" message records in the database and runs them through the processing engine.

ParameterDescription
Message CountHow many simulated messages to insert into the queue. Default: 2,000.
Timeout (seconds)The maximum time each processing wave is allowed to run before the next wave starts. Default: 1.5s.
Simulated Latency (ms)Artificial delay per processing chunk to mimic real-world network response time. Default: 50ms.

These parameters are saved in your browser session — navigating away and returning (even after re-authenticating) will not reset them during an active test.

Running a Simulation

  1. Set your desired parameters.
  2. Click Prepare Queue. This inserts the simulated messages into the database.
  3. Click Start Simulation. The processing engine begins dispatching in waves. The live stats panel updates every second.
  4. Monitor the Pending / Processing / Sent / Failed counters and the progress bar.
  5. Click Emergency Stop to halt the simulation at any time. Processing batches are safely rolled back to Pending.
  6. Once complete (or after stopping), click Clear Simulation Records to remove all test data from the database.
Always clear simulation records after testing. Simulated messages do not send real SMS but they do consume queue space and may interfere with live statistics if left behind.
📋 Recent SMS Logs

The bottom of the Developer Settings page shows the 50 most recent SMS log entries in a detailed table — including Recipient, Sender ID, Message Content, Status, and Timestamp. Click Refresh Logs to reload the latest data.

10. Tips & Best Practices
Get the most out of Concord on every campaign
▼

Before Every Campaign

  • Double-check your contact list for duplicate or malformed phone numbers — they cause failed deliveries and waste credits.
  • Use the Constituency page's "Exclude contacts without phone" toggle to automatically drop unreachable contacts before selecting.
  • Preview your message with personalisation tags using the Live SMS Preview panel on the Send page — confirm the tag replacement looks natural.
  • Keep messages under 160 characters where possible to use a single SMS part per recipient.

Personalisation Tags — Best Practices

  • Always test tags against a contact whose fields you know — verify [Firstname] produces a clean single word, not an empty string.
  • Avoid using [Position] in mass campaigns unless every recipient has a position recorded — it will display blank for contacts without one.
  • Tags are case-insensitive: [firstname] and [Firstname] both work.

Scheduling Reminders Efficiently

  • Schedule at least 2 minutes before the intended send time to account for the 1-minute processing cycle.
  • Check the Scheduled Queue page after creating reminders to confirm they appear with a Pending status.
  • Cancel and recreate a reminder if you need to change its time — there is no "edit time" option once saved.

Monitoring a Live Campaign

  • After sending, go to the Dashboard and watch the Live Queue Monitor. It auto-refreshes every second.
  • Expect a short delay (up to 1 minute) before messages move from Pending to Processing — this is the automated dispatch cycle running.
  • Visit Reports for a permanent log of every delivered message, including exact timestamps.

CSV Import Tips

  • Remove all special characters and merged cells from your spreadsheet before exporting as CSV.
  • Phone numbers should be in a consistent format — use +countrycode prefix throughout (no spaces, no dashes).
  • If your CSV includes other columns (address, age, etc.), the platform will ignore them — only name, phone, and group are imported.
The platform processes the message queue automatically every minute. You do not need to stay on any page or keep the browser open for messages to be sent after dispatch.
